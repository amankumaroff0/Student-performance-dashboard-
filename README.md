# Student-performance-dashboard-
The "War Room" Dashboard is a custom-engineered, AI-assisted study analytics platform I built in 11th grade to meticulously track my competitive exam preparation. Designed to replace scattered spreadsheets, this tool acts as a gamified command center for academic progress.  
​The Crimson Protocol UI: The dark black, red, and crimson color palette is a deliberate design choice. The dark background drastically reduces eye strain during long study sessions, while the crimson accents are used strategically to trigger psychological alertness and signal the critical importance of the data.
​Mock Test Lab: Features a dynamic performance trendline graph to visualize mock test score history, tracking progress from my starting baseline to current levels.  
​Strategic Syllabus Tracking: Chapters are categorized into Tiers A through D based on academic weight and personal interest. This section includes a 1-to-10 revision counter, "Marks Pending" and progress metrics, and "Mastered" toggles.
​Subject Mastery Breakdown: Expanding a subject like Chemistry or Physics reveals exact completion percentages and tracks exactly how many high-priority Tier A chapters are finished.
​Firepower Log: A quantitative daily output tracker that logs the exact volume of questions solved across Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics to ensure consistent daily effort.  
​Mission Deadline: A persistent, front-and-center countdown timer ticking down to the final exam to maintain focus and urgency.  
​Built iteratively using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with the help of Gemini and Antigravity, this project solved a personal productivity bottleneck and sparked my ongoing drive to merge product design with data analytics.
